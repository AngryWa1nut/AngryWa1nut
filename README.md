# Juhwan Park 

I'm an undergraduate researcher at **Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS)**, studying **Computer Science** and **English Linguistics & Language Technology**.

My research interests center on **Trustworthy AI**, particularly **Machine Unlearning**. I am interested in how machine learning systems can be updated to remove the influence of selected training data, and how we can assess whether that removal is effective.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Machine Unlearning**  
  Removing the influence of training data from learned models and understanding the behavior of models after unlearning.

- **Trustworthy AI**  
  Developing and evaluating machine learning systems whose behavior and limitations can be understood and trusted.

My academic background spans computer science and linguistics, with previous interests in natural language processing, computer vision, and quantum machine learning.
